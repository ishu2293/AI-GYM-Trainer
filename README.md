# AI Real-Time GYM Trainer

An AI-powered real-time workout coach and fitness assistant that uses computer vision, pose estimation, Large Language Models, and Text-to-Speech to analyze exercises, provide live audio coaching, track workout progress, and generate personalized diet and fitness plans.

The application captures webcam input to detect body posture in real time, counts repetitions, flags incorrect form, delivers proactive voice coaching, visualizes workout consistency on an interactive dashboard, and creates tailored workout and meal plans.

---

## Features

- **Real-Time Pose Estimation**: 33-point human pose tracking powered by MediaPipe Pose Landmarker.
- **Form Analysis & Correction**: Exercise-specific angle and alignment tracking to detect common form errors (e.g., shallow squats, sagging hips, torso swing).
- **Automated Rep Counting**: State-machine repetition counting with phase detection across sets.
- **AI Voice Coaching**: Proactive audio feedback and motivation using Groq LLMs and Google Text-to-Speech (gTTS).
- **Interactive Progress Dashboard**: Workout analytics built with Plotly, including total reps, total sets, weekly progress trends, and exercise distribution charts.
- **AI Diet & Workout Planner**: Customized weekly training splits and daily meal plans generated by LLMs based on fitness goals, diet preferences, available equipment, and dietary restrictions.
- **Workout History & Persistence**: SQLite-backed storage for completed workouts, user profiles, and generated fitness plans.
- **Low-Latency Streaming**: Browser-based video and audio processing using Streamlit WebRTC.
- **User Authentication**: Simple login and user session management.
- **Multi-Page Navigation**: Streamlit interface with dedicated views for **Workout**, **Progress Dashboard**, and **AI Planner**.

## How It Works

### Real-Time Workout & Voice Coaching Pipeline

```text
Webcam
   │
   ▼
WebRTC Video Stream
   │
   ▼
MediaPipe Pose Landmarker
   │
   ▼
Exercise Detector (Angle & Phase Calculation)
   │
   ▼
Form Evaluation & Rep Counter
   │
   ▼
AI Coach (Groq LLM)
   │
   ▼
Text-to-Speech (gTTS)
   │
   ▼
WebRTC Audio / Autoplay
   │
   ▼
Real-Time Voice Coaching
```

### Analytics & Planning Workflows

- **Progress Dashboard**: Queries SQLite workout records, aggregates metrics with Pandas, and displays interactive charts using Plotly Express.
- **AI Fitness Planner**: Collects user profile details (goals, diet, schedule, equipment, allergies) and prompts the Groq LLM to generate structured weekly workout routines and daily meal plans saved directly to SQLite.

## Tech Stack

### Programming Language
- Python 3.10+

### AI & Large Language Models
- **Groq API**: High-speed inference using Llama / open-source models (`openai/gpt-oss-20b` for coaching feedback, `openai/gpt-oss-120b` for diet & workout planning)

### Computer Vision & Pose Tracking
- **MediaPipe**: Pose Landmarker (`pose_landmarker_full.task`)
- **OpenCV**: Image transformations and landmark visualization

### Voice & Audio
- **gTTS** (Google Text-to-Speech): Generates dynamic audio feedback
- **WebRTC Audio Streaming / HTML5 Audio**: Real-time voice playback

### Web Framework & Streaming
- **Streamlit**: Web interface and application state management
- **streamlit-webrtc**: Real-time WebRTC video and audio streaming

### Data Processing & Visualization
- **Plotly Express**: Interactive workout charts and performance analytics
- **Pandas**: Workout data aggregation and manipulation
- **NumPy**: Vectorized angle and coordinate geometry calculations

### Database
- **SQLite**: Local relational database (`data.db`) storing users, workouts, and fitness plans

## Application Navigation & Usage

The application provides three primary modules accessible from the sidebar navigation:

### 1. Workout Mode
- **Configure Session**: Select the exercise, target sets, and target reps in the sidebar.
- **Start Workout**: Click **Start Session** and allow camera access.
- **Real-Time Guidance**: As you exercise, the camera feed displays skeleton overlays while the sidebar updates your repetition count and live angles.
- **Voice Coaching**: The AI coach speaks real-time correction cues when form issues are detected and encourages you between sets.

### 2. Progress Dashboard
- **Overall Statistics**: View total reps completed, total sets performed, and total logged sessions.
- **Weekly Progress Chart**: Interactive Plotly bar chart tracking reps completed each week.
- **Exercise Breakdown**: Visual breakdown of total reps per exercise type.
- **Workout Logs**: Chronological table of past workouts with timestamps, sets, and reps.

### 3. AI Diet & Workout Planner
- **User Profile**: Input your fitness goal (muscle gain, weight loss, etc.), diet preference (vegetarian, vegan, eggetarian, non-vegetarian), location (home/gym), fitness level, age, height, weight, workout frequency, and available equipment.
- **Allergies & Restrictions**: Specify food allergies or dietary restrictions.
- **Plan Generation**: Click **Generate & Save My Plan** to receive a structured weekly workout schedule and daily meal plan.
- **Saved Plans**: Generated plans are stored in SQLite and can be reloaded at any time.

## Exercise Metrics & Analysis

### Squats
- **Tracked Joints**: Hip, Knee, Ankle
- **Metrics**: Knee Angle, Back Angle, Depth Status (`GOOD`, `TOO HIGH`)
- **Common Feedback**: "Your squat is not deep enough", "Avoid leaning too far forward"

### Push-ups
- **Tracked Joints**: Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist, Hip, Ankle
- **Metrics**: Elbow Angle, Body Alignment, Hip Status (`NORMAL`, `SAGGING`, `PIKED UP`)
- **Common Feedback**: "Keep your body in a straight line", "Do not let your hips sag"

### Biceps Curls
- **Tracked Joints**: Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist, Hip
- **Metrics**: Elbow Angle, Shoulder Stability, Torso Swing (`STABLE`, `SWINGING`)
- **Common Feedback**: "Keep your elbows pinned to your sides", "Avoid swinging your torso"

### Shoulder Press
- **Tracked Joints**: Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist, Hip
- **Metrics**: Elbow Angle, Arm Extension (`FULL`, `PARTIAL`), Back Arch (`Normal`, `Slight Arch`, `Excessive Arch`)
- **Common Feedback**: "Lock out at the top", "Keep your core tight and avoid arching your lower back"

### Lunges
- **Tracked Joints**: Hip, Knee, Ankle
- **Metrics**: Front Knee Angle, Torso Angle, Balance Status (`GOOD`, `OFF BALANCE`)
- **Common Feedback**: "Keep your front knee aligned over your ankle", "Maintain an upright torso"

## Database Schema

The SQLite database (`data.db`) consists of three tables:

1. **`users`**: User authentication credentials and profile identifiers.
2. **`exercises`**: Logged workout sessions (`user_id`, `exercise_name`, `reps`, `sets`, `time`, `created_at`).
3. **`fitness_plans`**: Saved AI-generated fitness profiles and plans (`user_id`, `profile_json`, `plan_text`, `updated_at`).
